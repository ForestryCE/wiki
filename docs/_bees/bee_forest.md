The **Forest Bee** *(also as a Drone, a Princess, a Queen, and Larva)* is one of the six [Mundane Bee](../Mundane+Bees) species in Forestry:CE. It is recognizeable by its light blue outline and yellow stripes. You can only obtain a **Forest Princess** and a **Forest Drone** from breaking a [Forest Hive](../Hives) with a [Scoop](../Scoop).

It is the most common pest in forests. The Forest Bee, as well as the Meadow Bee, are both known to be docile, not causing people even the smallest of stings.
<span class="reference">- Forester's Manual "Mundane Bees"</span>
